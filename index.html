    About & privacy

    Commands travel through the free public relay ntfy.sh, addressed by the secret code from your TV's QR code. Anyone with that code could control Prestige; create a new code on the TV (Settings › Phone remote) to revoke it. Links you send pass through the relay.

    Works while Prestige is open on the TV. To switch the TV on or off, use Samsung's SmartThings app. Tip: add this page to your home screen; on Android you can then share links straight to it.
